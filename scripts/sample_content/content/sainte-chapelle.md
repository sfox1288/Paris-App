A jewel-box Gothic chapel built by Louis IX in the 1240s to house the Crown of Thorns. The upper chapel's stained glass — 15 windows depicting 1,113 biblical scenes — is among the finest medieval glass in the world. Go on a sunny day if possible. Security line shared with the Conciergerie next door can be slow; combo ticket saves time.
