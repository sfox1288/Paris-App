Housed in a former Beaux-Arts railway station, the Orsay covers Western art from 1848 to 1914 — the gap between the Louvre and the Pompidou. The Impressionist galleries on the top floor (Monet, Renoir, Degas, Van Gogh) are the headline draw. The famous golden clock face overlooks the Seine. Pair with the Orangerie across the river for a complete Impressionist day.
