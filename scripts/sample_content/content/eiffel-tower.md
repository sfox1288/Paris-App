Built for the 1889 World's Fair and originally meant to stand for only 20 years, the Eiffel Tower is now Paris's defining landmark. Three levels are open to visitors, with the summit at 276 m. Best photos are from Trocadéro across the river or from the Champ de Mars below. Hourly sparkle on the hour after dark.
