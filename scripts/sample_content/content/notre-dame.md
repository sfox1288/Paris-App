Notre-Dame de Paris, the medieval Catholic cathedral on the Île de la Cité, has stood since the 12th century. After the 2019 fire, restoration is ongoing — exterior viewing is the main draw, with the reopened interior accessible by timed entry. The square in front (the parvis) is the official 'Point Zero' from which all distances in France are measured.
